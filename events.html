What to expect

Open Testing Weekend

Every gamemode, every region. Extra testers are online all weekend so queues move fast — a good time to get your first tier or update an old one.

Ladder nights

Two hours of one gamemode on a live ladder. Win to climb, lose to drop. Ladder results are separate from official tiers, but testers watch them.

Team clashes

Sixteen teams, single elimination, best-of-five. Sign-up happens in-game a week before the event.
