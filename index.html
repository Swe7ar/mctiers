Frequently asked questions

01 What is MCTiers? +
MCTiers is a Minecraft PvP tier list. It ranks players in eight gamemodes (Vanilla/Crystal PvP, UHC, Pot, NethOP, SMP, Sword, Axe and Mace) from High Tier 1 to Low Tier 5, and combines those tiers into one overall leaderboard.
02 What do HT1, LT1 and the other tiers mean? +
Each gamemode has five tiers, and Tier 1 is the best. Every tier is split into High (HT) and Low (LT), so HT1 is the highest rank a player can hold and LT5 the lowest. The full order is HT1, LT1, HT2, LT2 and so on down to LT5.
03 How do I get tier tested? +
Join 130.78.218.210:25606 on Java or Bedrock and type /tiertest. Choose the gamemode you want to be ranked in and your region to join that gamemode's testing queue. When a tester in your region is free, you're moved to a private arena with the official kit, and your tier is added to your MCTiers profile after the test.
04 How is the overall leaderboard calculated? +
Every tier is worth points: HT1 60, LT1 45, HT2 30, LT2 20, HT3 10, LT3 6, HT4 4, LT4 3, HT5 2 and LT5 1. A player's points from all gamemodes are added together and the overall leaderboard is sorted by that total. When a player has reached a higher peak tier, the peak is what counts.
05 What are titles like Combat Master? +
Titles reflect a player's total points: Rookie below 10 points, Combat Novice from 10, Combat Cadet from 20, Combat Specialist from 50, Combat Ace from 100, Combat Master from 250 and Combat Grandmaster from 400.
06 How do I look up a player's tiers? +
Use the search box at the top of any page (press / to jump to it) and enter a Minecraft username. The profile shows the player's overall placement, points, title and every tier they hold.
07 How do accounts work? +
Players are added by the owner in assets/js/roster.js with their real Minecraft name, so the skin and face load automatically. Everyone sees the same roster. The admin (username admin) is the only one who can set or change tiers.
08 Does this work without internet? +
Yes. Gamemodes, points and titles are bundled with the site, and accounts are stored locally. Only the optional 3D skin renders come from the network — without a connection the site draws a generated pixel avatar instead.
